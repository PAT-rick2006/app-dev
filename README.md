# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man 🕷️

## About the Movie
My favorite movie is **Spider-Man**. It is a superhero movie about Peter Parker, an ordinary student who gains amazing powers after being bitten by a radioactive spider.

## Main Character
- **Name:** Peter Parker
- **Superhero Name:** Spider-Man
- **Special Powers:**
  - Super strength
  - Wall climbing
  - Spider-sense
  - Web shooting
  - Super agility

## Why I Like Spider-Man
I like Spider-Man because he is brave, helpful, and responsible. Even though he faces many challenges, he always tries to protect other people.

## My Favorite Quote
> "With great power comes great responsibility."
